IACT-registered practice

A confidential space to work through what's weighing on you.

Counselling and psychotherapy at Pegasus is for individuals, students and athletes working through stress, anxiety, confidence, motivation, or performance blocks — not only for people in crisis.

Book a confidential session Ask a question first

Who this is for

Students, athletes & working adults

  • Students managing exam stress, motivation or school transitions
  • Athletes working through performance anxiety, burnout or injury setbacks
  • Adults navigating work stress, confidence or life changes
  • Anyone who wants structured support, not just a listening ear

How sessions work

In-person or online

  • Sessions held at the academy or over video call
  • First session focuses on understanding your goals, not diagnosis
  • Approach draws on sport & performance psychology as well as talk therapy
  • Everything discussed is confidential between you and your therapist

Common questions

Before you book

What happens in the first session?

You and your therapist talk through what brought you in and what you'd like to be different. There's no obligation to continue if it doesn't feel right — fit matters.

Do you work with teens?

Yes. Sessions with minors involve an initial conversation with a parent or guardian about goals and boundaries around confidentiality.

Is this only for athletes?

No — our sport psychology background shapes our approach, but counselling clients come from all walks of life, not only sport.

How do I book?

Use Book a Class/Session above, or message us via the contact page and we'll match you with a therapist.

Corporate & School Programmes

Resilience training for teams, and enrichment for schools.

The same pairing of physical challenge and mental skills coaching, delivered to organisations and MOE classrooms rather than individuals.

For schools

MOE-registered enrichment

Co-curricular and enrichment activities combining boxing fundamentals with character education — discipline, focus and composure, built through structured physical practice.

  • MOE-registered instructors for co-curricular activities
  • Character & resilience modules alongside boxing skills
  • Runs as CCA blocks, enrichment days, or School Holiday Camps

For organisations

Team resilience workshops

Half- or full-day sessions that pair a physical challenge (boxing-based drills, no experience required) with psychology-backed debriefs on stress, communication and pressure.

  • Offsites and team-building days
  • Stress & resilience workshops for teams under pressure
  • Scales from small teams to whole departments
